<div class="lang-block lang-zh" markdown="1">

我们在三种噪声模型下比较了两类外码。使用 GKP 测量的连续读数进行译码时，两类码的有限尺寸逻辑错误率曲线都在更高的噪声强度处相交。在考虑位移误差如何沿综合征提取线路传播的模型中，所测 BB 码序列的交叉点约为 σ = 0.212，tricycle 码序列约为 σ = 0.142。

</div>

<div class="lang-block lang-en" markdown="1">

We benchmarked the two outer-code families under three noise models. Using continuous GKP readouts in the decoder shifted the finite-size crossings of the logical error curves to higher noise levels for both families. When the simulation tracked displacement errors through the syndrome-extraction circuit, the estimated crossings were σ = 0.212 for the selected BB codes and σ = 0.142 for the selected tricycle codes.

</div>
