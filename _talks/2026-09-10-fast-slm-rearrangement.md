<div class="lang-block lang-zh" markdown="1">

这次报告总结了我暑期在快速 SLM 光阱重排方面的工作：衍射效率、响应时间和稳态波动测试，光路与同步拍摄，光学校准与相机反馈，以及 CNN、三次迭代 Dirichlet WGS 和 Fast WGS 的实拍对比。报告还展示了全流程耗时、光强分布随时间的变化，以及单个光阱的光强轨迹。

<div class="lang-block lang-en" markdown="1">

This talk summarizes my summer work on fast SLM-based optical-trap rearrangement: diffraction efficiency, switching response and steady-state fluctuations; the optical setup and synchronized imaging; optical calibration and camera feedback; and camera comparisons of CNN, three-iteration Dirichlet WGS and Fast WGS. It also presents full-sequence timing, evolving intensity distributions and individual-trap intensity traces.
